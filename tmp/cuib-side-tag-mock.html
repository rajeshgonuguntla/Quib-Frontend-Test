What is Artificial Intelligence?

Overview
READING · 8 min
SUMMARY

In this lesson, you will gain a foundational understanding of Artificial Intelligence (AI) by defining what it is and exploring its pervasive presence in our daily lives. You will learn how AI enables computers and systems to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence, such as recognizing faces, understanding speech, and making recommendations.

KEY TAKEAWAYS

AI is technology enabling computers to perform tasks requiring human intelligence.

Everyday applications of AI include facial recognition, voice assistants, and recommendation systems.
